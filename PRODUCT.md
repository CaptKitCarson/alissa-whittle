# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Three audiences carrying roughly equal weight, addressed by the same site:

- **Keys locals and transplants nearing retirement** (55–70, ~$500k–$3M investable) — value a real person within driving distance of MM 92. Place matters.
- **Out-of-state referrals, higher net worth** (~$1M+) — found Alissa through someone they trust. Fine with phone/Zoom. Care most about the fiduciary stance and seventeen years of context.
- **Mid-career professionals** (35–55, accumulating) — want planning, not just investment products. Need education before they're ready to talk.

Common context: they're deciding whether to trust a person with money. The site is one stop in a longer evaluation that almost always includes Googling Alissa, checking BrokerCheck, and asking someone.

## Product Purpose

A credibility surface for Alissa Whittle's independent fiduciary practice under LPL Financial, based in Tavernier, FL and licensed across nineteen states. The site exists to convert a warm visitor — referral, local search, or post-meeting reference — into a scheduled conversation. Success is one action: booking a call.

## Brand Personality

**Trusted · warm · sharp.**

Voice: first-name human, fluent in the work, never performing expertise. Comfortable saying "your desk" and "her desk." Specific about place, credentials, and process; vague about nothing.

Emotional goal: a visitor closes the tab thinking *this is a real person I'd want to talk to about real money*, not *this is a professional services website.*

## Anti-references

Explicitly not big-bank corporate:

- Stock-photo handshakes and skylines.
- Navy + gold, generic "Your goals, our plan" copy.
- Compliance theater that overwhelms the human.
- Anything that could appear on a Schwab or Edward Jones landing page without changing a word.

Adjacent traps to avoid by extension: suburban-realtor circular headshots and gradient buttons; robo-advisor neon-sans-and-dashboard energy; tropical-Florida kitsch (palm trees, sunset gradients).

## Design Principles

1. **The room, not the building.** The site should feel like Alissa's desk in Tavernier — specific, human, present — not a corporate floor. Detail and specificity beat scale.
2. **Warmth carries weight.** Trust does not have to look stiff. Warm tone is the active differentiator from the big-bank category, not a softener applied on top of competence.
3. **Sharpness in the small print.** Credentials, licensing map, FINRA disclosures, broker-dealer footers — treated with the same care as the headline. Compliance is design, not afterthought.
4. **One voice for three audiences.** Local retiree, out-of-state HNW, and mid-career planner must all feel addressed without splintering the site into segmented landing pages. Cohesion is the asset.
5. **Conversation is the conversion.** Every page leads to one scheduled call. No lead magnets, no email-gated calculators, no chatbots.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Visible focus states on every interactive element, contrast ratios verified, keyboard navigation tested across the licensing map and form, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected throughout. No specific known user need flagged.
